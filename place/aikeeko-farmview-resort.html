About Aikeeko Farmview Resort

Aikeeko FarmView Resort first gained social media popularity in late 2021 as a picturesque floral farm destination. Managed by its hands-on CEO, Evangeline Capundag, the property has evolved into a fully equipped mountain resort designed for "farmcations," eco-tourism, and digital detoxing.
