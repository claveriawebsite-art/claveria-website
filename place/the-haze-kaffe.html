About The Haze Kaffe

The Haze Kaffe is an eco-tourism mountainside retreat, café, and mini-resort — a premium sanctuary for travelers exploring Route 955, nestled right on the Gingoog–Claveria mountain border, intentionally removed from urban noise and pollution.
