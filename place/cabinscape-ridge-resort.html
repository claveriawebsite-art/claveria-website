About Cabinscape Ridge Resort

Cabinscape Ridge Resort quickly became one of Claveria's most photogenic mountain destinations. Built specifically to provide an aesthetic, Nordic-inspired escape from city life, it leans into the naturally cold, mist-covered environment of the Misamis Oriental highlands.
