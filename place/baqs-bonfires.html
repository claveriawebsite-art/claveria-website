About Baqs&Bonfires

Baqs&Bonfires is a peaceful mountain-side camping and staycation destination located in Sitio Sumpong, Barangay Madaguing, Claveria, Misamis Oriental. Surrounded by refreshing mountain scenery and beautiful natural views, it offers visitors a relaxing place to unwind and enjoy quality time with family and friends — cozy cabin accommodations, outdoor bonding, and memorable evenings around a bonfire.
