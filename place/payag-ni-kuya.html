About Payag ni Kuya

Payag ni Kuya is a cozy countryside retreat in Barangay Patrocinio, Claveria, Misamis Oriental. Surrounded by lush greenery, refreshing mountain air, and beautiful natural scenery, it features traditional nipa-style huts and camping areas — a great destination for families, friends, and travelers who want to escape the busy city life.
