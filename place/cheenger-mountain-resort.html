About Chenenger Mountain Resort

Chenenger Mountain Resort (managed in part by the Flores family) was established in late 2022 to provide an affordable, unpretentious retreat in the growing eco-tourism hub of Barangay Lanise, Claveria — a highly practical, home-style communal escape tailored for traditional Filipino group gatherings like family outings and barkada staycations.
